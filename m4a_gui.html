About Audio Forge…

Audio Forge

Version 1.0.0 · Desktop audio utility

Convert MP4, MP3, FLAC and M4A. Edit tags and artwork. All processing runs on your computer using FFmpeg.

Open a file, choose the target format, then check Tags & artwork before saving. Matching formats edit tags without re-encoding.
